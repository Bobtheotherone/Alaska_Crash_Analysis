## Two disclosed quirks

* **Generator ≠ artifact-commit ≠ release.** The numbers were produced at `e55d6f1`, committed at
  `e17c263`, released at `e06e803`. Any sentence collapsing these into "the commit" is imprecise;
  the v3 paper's §11 did exactly that and is corrected in v3.1.
* **BUNDLE-CRLF-001.** The v3.x bundle writer hashed its in-memory LF content but materialised
  files with platform newlines (CRLF on Windows), so `manifest.json → artifact_sha256` matches the
  on-disk originals only after CRLF→LF normalisation. `DEID_TRANSFORM.md` records both hashes per
  file and the normalisation under which the chain closes. The v4 bundle writer writes the exact
  bytes it hashes.
